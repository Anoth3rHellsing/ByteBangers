## Full Description

**🎵 ByteBangers — Your 16-Bit Retro Wave Music Studio 🎵**

Step into the neon-soaked world of 16-bit music creation. ByteBangers is a powerful yet intuitive digital audio workstation (DAW) that lets you craft authentic chiptune, SNES-era, and Genesis-style music — all wrapped in a stunning Retro Wave aesthetic.

### ✨ KEY FEATURES

** 8-Track Step Sequencer**
- 16-step grid per zone with click-to-toggle cells
- Visual playhead shows exactly where you are in the beat
- Beat markers every 4 steps for easy rhythm building

**🎛️ 8 Built-In 16-Bit Instruments**
- **Lead** — Square wave with chorus detune for that classic melody sound
- **Bass** — Warm sawtooth with low-pass filter for deep grooves
- **Pad** — Lush triangle wave with slow attack for atmospheric chords
- **Arp** — Fast-decay square wave perfect for arpeggios
- **Kick** — Sine wave with pitch-envelope drop for punchy drums
- **Snare** — Noise burst with bandpass character
- **Hi-Hat** — High-frequency noise for crisp percussion
- **FX** — Resonant sawtooth sweep for transitions and effects

**🌊 Multi-Zone Song Arrangement (Up to 99 Zones!)**
- Chain zones together to build full songs, not just short loops
- Each zone has its own independent 16-step pattern
- Switch between zones instantly during editing
- Playback flows seamlessly through all zones in sequence
- Add or remove zones on the fly

**🎚️ Per-Track Mixer**
- Volume control per instrument
- Stereo panning (left/right placement)
- Mute and Solo buttons for each track
- Instrument selector — swap sounds per track instantly

**💾 Export to WAV**
- Render your entire multi-zone song to a standard 16-bit PCM WAV file
- 44,100 Hz sample rate for professional quality
- Automatic normalization for consistent volume
- Trailing silence trimmed automatically
- Download-ready — share your creations anywhere

**🎨 Stunning Retro Wave Interface**
- Synthwave/outrun aesthetic with sunset gradients
- Animated perspective grid floor
- Retro sun with color-shifting glow
- Twinkling star field background
- Chrome metallic title text
- Neon pink and cyan accent colors
- Glass-morphism panels with backdrop blur

**⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts**
- **Space** — Play / Stop
- **Z, X, C, V** — Live note input (C4, D4, E4, F4)
- **← →** — Switch between zones
- Full transport control without touching the mouse

**💾 Auto-Save**
- Your song is automatically saved to local storage
- Never lose your work — it's there when you come back
- Supports legacy single-pattern migration

### 🔧 TECHNICAL DETAILS

- **Audio Engine:** Web Audio API with native oscillator synthesis
- **Sound Design:** Bitcrusher effect for authentic 16-bit DAC emulation
- **No External Samples:** All sounds generated in real-time — zero dependencies
- **ADSR Envelopes:** Full attack, decay, sustain, release per instrument
- **Resonant Filters:** Low-pass, band-pass, and high-pass filtering
- **Master Chain:** Compressor + limiter for clean output
- **Offline Rendering:** WAV export uses OfflineAudioContext for precise timing
